# Strata 2.0 film — v4 spec: reference-style transitions + flowing background

Client: "make the transitions like in this reference video, and let the background change by flowing (переливанием)".
Reference: "Higgsfield for Figma" (frames in `film/ref_figma/`, see the contact sheets `t1..t4.jpg` and `s5a..c.jpg`). Its transition grammar:
- **No hard cuts. One continuous canvas.** Scenes overlap; the outgoing content leaves while the incoming content arrives.
- **Fly-through / zoom-past:** the outgoing hero scales up fast toward the camera and dissolves in its own motion blur (title at 2.4–2.7 s in the ref).
- **Object carry-over:** a key object (often held by a cursor) travels into the next scene and becomes part of it; everything else whips out with a strong directional smear while the new layout slides in around the carried object (ref 7.4–8.4, 11.4–11.8, 28.3–28.7).
- **Grow-from-a-point:** new elements fly in from a tiny point, growing with blur, and land with a soft settle (ref 10.7–10.9).
- **Smears are motion blur:** moves into/out of a transition are fast with long eases (`E.in(2..3)` out, `E.out(3)`/`E.soft` in); the render adds 12-sub-frame motion blur, so don't fake blur except a light x-blur (≤ 10 px) on whips.
